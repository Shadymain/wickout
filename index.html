Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

WICKOUT does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

On your phone only. Your player name, colour, settings, records and trophies are saved on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Bluetooth. Nearby play uses Bluetooth to connect directly to other phones running WICKOUT close by. Only game information (your player name, colour and in-game moves) is exchanged between those phones, for the length of the game. Nothing goes over the internet and nothing is kept afterwards.

Online (Game Center). Online play uses Apple's Game Center to find players and connect phones. Apple handles your Game Center sign-in under Apple's privacy policy. During a match only game information (your WICKOUT name, colour and moves) goes to the other players' phones. WICKOUT has no server of its own and keeps nothing.

Children. WICKOUT collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue on GitHub.

Nearby won't connect? Make sure Bluetooth is on and WICKOUT is allowed to use it (Settings → WICKOUT → Bluetooth). Keep WICKOUT open on every phone during a round. Airplane Mode is fine as long as Bluetooth stays on.
